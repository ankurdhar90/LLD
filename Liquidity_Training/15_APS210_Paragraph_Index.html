How this pack was checked

  • Coverage of the standard. Every numbered paragraph of APS 210 has its own companion entry: main body 1 to 72, Attachment A 1 to 66, Attachment B 1 to 6 and Attachment C 1 to 42, plus Tables 1 to 4 of Attachment A, Table 1 of Attachment C and footnotes 1 to 29. A script confirms none is missing, merged or out of order, and that each entry has all five parts.
  • Rates and factors. The tables of the standard were read from the published PDF by the position of each number on the page, because plain text extraction separates rates from their rows. The one-page LCR rate card and NSFR factor card in document 13 were then compared with them line by line.
  • Arithmetic. The Example Bank figures and the practice case were computed by script before writing began. Every division and percentage calculation written out in the documents was recomputed afterwards.
  • Links. Every paragraph citation in the pack links to its companion entry, and every link between pages was tested.

What is rule, what is convention, and what was not verified

Rules cite a paragraph of APS 210 and can be relied on as a restatement of the standard. The standard itself always takes precedence.

Example Bank conventions and typical practice are illustrations, not rules. They include: the Board appetite (LCR 120%, NSFR 110%) and management triggers (125%, 115%); every timetable and cut-off time; tolerances; team and committee names; who prepares, reviews and signs; severity levels; record retention periods; the convention that on-line accounts are always scored; the pass mark for the assessment. Your bank's own policies replace all of these.

Verified on official sites (by the writer of document 11, with sources listed there): Reporting Standard ARS 210.0 is in force from 4 April 2023; its forms, who files which, reporting levels, and due dates (quarterly returns within 35 calendar days; the annual funding plan form within 35 days of financial year end); APRA Connect roles and file types; the in-force version of APS 330 and its liquidity disclosure clauses; APRA's liquidity FAQ; the phase-out of the Committed Liquidity Facility to zero as at 1 January 2023; the July 2025 changes to APS 210.

Simplifications in the examples

  • Example Bank has one legal entity and one currency, so Level 1 equals Level 2 and the Australian dollar LCR equals the all-currencies LCR.
  • All of its liquid assets are HQLA1, so no haircut or cap applies in the base case.
  • Derivatives are ignored in the base NSFR; the practice case includes them.
  • "Other liabilities" are assumed to have nothing contractually due within 30 days.
  • Wholesale debt under one year is assumed to be held by financial institutions.
