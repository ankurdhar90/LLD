01 Syllabus and document design

Purpose: Set out what you will learn, in what order, and what each document in the pack is designed to contain. Time: 20 minutes now; return to it at the start of each week.

What you will be able to do at the end

  1. Explain liquidity, the LCR and the NSFR to someone outside the bank.
  2. Find any requirement in APS 210 and say what it means for our team.
  3. Take raw balances and classify each one correctly for the LCR and the NSFR.
  4. Produce the daily LCR and the monthly NSFR, explain every movement, and evidence your work.
  5. Prepare the liquidity returns to APRA and reconcile them.
  6. Recognise a problem early and escalate it correctly.

How to find the training for any part of the standard

  1. Note the paragraph number in the PDF, and whether it is in the main body or in Attachment A, B or C.
  2. Open document 15 and find that number.
  3. The first link goes to the companion entry, which restates the paragraph, explains it in everyday words and says what our team does about it.
  4. The other links go to every document that applies that paragraph in practice.

How this pack was written and checked

The controller designed the syllabus and each document's contents, set the accuracy rules, and built the worked examples with their arithmetic checked by script. Eight writers drafted the documents from the text of APS 210. The controller then marked them: every paragraph of the standard must have a companion entry, every rate was compared with the standard's tables read directly from the PDF, and every calculation was recomputed. Document 15 lists anything that could not be verified.
