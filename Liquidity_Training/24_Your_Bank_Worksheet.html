24 Your bank: the worksheet that replaces Example Bank

Purpose: Everything in this pack that is labelled "Example Bank convention" or "typical practice" is a placeholder. This worksheet lists each placeholder and where to find your real bank's version, so you can replace the illustration with fact.

Before you start: Documents 02 to 12.

Time: Two to three weeks alongside your normal work. Bring the completed sheet to your controller.

From the controller: No training pack can know your bank. The rules are the same everywhere; the limits, systems, people and judgements are not. A new analyst who has filled in this sheet knows more about how the bank really works than most people after a year.

How to use it

Print it or copy it. For each row, find the answer, write down where you found it (the policy name and section, the system, or the person's role) and the date. If the answer does not exist, that is a finding: raise it.

1. What kind of ADI are we

Question Why it matters Where to look Your answer and source
Are we an LCR ADI or an MLH ADI? Decides which attachment of APS 210 applies (APS 210 paras 52 to 54) APRA's classification letter; the liquidity policy
Locally incorporated or a foreign ADI (branch)? 100% or 40% LCR minimum; whether the NSFR applies (APS 210 paras 55, 59) Annual report; the liquidity policy
Has APRA set a higher minimum LCR or NSFR for us? It replaces 100% as our real floor (APS 210 paras 56, 61) APRA correspondence, held by the controller or Risk
What is in our Level 1 and Level 2 group? Which entities each number covers (APS 210 paras 3, 4) Group structure chart; the capital and liquidity policies
Do we have securitisation vehicles, and how are they treated? Included in LCR cash flows; may be excluded from the NSFR (APS 210 para 5) Treasury; the securitisation policy
Which currencies are material? Separate analysis by currency; AUD and all-currencies LCR (APS 210 paras 43, 55) Liquidity policy; the latest return
Has APRA granted any adjustment or exclusion? It changes how a rule applies to us (APS 210 paras 71, 72) APRA correspondence

8. The balance sheet itself

Take the latest month-end and rebuild these for your bank, as you did for Example Bank:

  1. The balance sheet in the layout of document 02, with every line classified.
  2. The LCR working paper of document 08. Does it reproduce the reported figure?
  3. The NSFR working paper of document 09. Does it reconcile to the ledger?
  4. The five largest movements in each ratio over the last quarter, explained.
  5. The lineage of one number you choose, traced from source system to return, using document 16 as the template.

When you have finished

Review the sheet with your controller. Anything you could not find, or found to be undocumented, goes on a list with an owner and a date. That list is the most useful thing a new analyst produces in the first three months.

Watch out: This worksheet will contain internal information about your bank. Keep the completed copy inside the bank's systems, not with this training pack.
