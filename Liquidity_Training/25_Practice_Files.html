25 Practice files

Purpose: Give you the practice case as data you can work in, not just read.

Before you start: Document 14 (Exercises and assessments), Level 3.

Time: 2 to 3 hours for a first attempt.

The files

File What it is Use it to

15.00 / 13.04 = 115.0% and 79.45 / 61.44 = 129.3%.

How the columns work

  • LCR role is one of HQLA1, HQLA2A, HQLA2B, Outflow, Inflow or None.
  • LCR rate means different things by role: for HQLA it is the share of market value that counts after the haircut (Att A paras 9 to 12); for an outflow it is the run-off rate (Att A Table 3); for an inflow it is the inflow rate (Att A paras 54 to 66).
  • NSFR role is ASF (available stable funding: liabilities and equity), RSF (required stable funding: assets and commitments) or None.
  • A loan balance and the repayments due on it within 30 days are separate rows on purpose: the balance drives the NSFR, the repayments drive the LCR.
  • Trade finance and guarantees also appear twice: the monthly average drives the LCR (Att A Table 3) and the 12-month total drives the NSFR (Att C para 42).

Three exercises to try in the workbook

  1. The incident. On the Answers sheet, change the LCR rate of item n (wholesale debt, 30 days to under 6 months) from 0% to 100%, as if it had moved inside the 30-day window. The LCR falls to 94.7%, a breach (Att A para 5). Document 12 (Controls, incidents and escalation) walks through what happens next.
  2. Fix it. With the incident still applied, add A$1.0bn to item 2 (the RBA balance) and A$1.0bn to item p (debt of 1 year or more). The LCR recovers to 101.0%.
  3. A classification error. Change item j (superannuation fund deposits) to a 40% rate, as if someone had treated the funds as corporates rather than financial institutions (APS 210 para 10). See how much the LCR is overstated, and why that mistake matters.

From the controller: Build the calculation yourself from the raw file at least once before you rely on anyone's spreadsheet, including this one. You only understand a ratio once you have rebuilt it.
